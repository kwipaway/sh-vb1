VETSUCCESS SUPPORT SERVICES

Get help understanding and managing your VA education benefits.

  • VA BENEFITS & VOCATIONAL REHAB

      One-on-one assistance with navigating and applying for VA educational benefits, disability compensation, life insurance, and home loan guarantees.

  • ACADEMIC & CAREER COUNSELING

      Get support choosing a major, exploring career paths, and connecting with SHSU faculty, Career Success Center, and community resources to build your future.

  • MENTAL HEALTH & READJUSTMENT SERVIC

      Referral assistance for VA mental health services, Vet Center programs, and campus counseling tailored for veterans and their families.

  • TRANSITION & COMMUNITY OUTREACH

      We collaborate with Vet Centers to host outreach, community education, and transition activities—creating a supportive campus environment.
