# Requirements
## Objectives

Redesign the existing website to make information easy to find, better organized, and accessible across devices—addressing the current issue of users being unable to locate what they need and overwhelming staff with inquiries.
Develop an AI-powered chatbot that assists visitors by answering common questions, guiding them to relevant information, and reducing the overall workload of staff.
Use AI tools throughout the project to improve content development, design quality, problem‑solving, and overall project efficiency.


## Grading Criteria

### Content (35%)
Relevance and Quality: Content is accurate, complete, well‑researched, and appropriate for the Veterans Benefits Processing Team. (10%)
Clarity and Readability: Content is clearly written, logically organized, and free of grammatical or factual errors. (10%)
Engagement: Content uses appropriate visuals, multimedia, and formatting to maintain user interest. (10%)
SEO Optimization: Content includes effective keywords, headings, and metadata to improve search visibility. (5%)

### Design (35%)
Visual Appeal: Website design is clean, modern, professional, and consistent. (10%)
User Experience (UX): Navigation is intuitive, with a structure that makes information easy to find. (10%)
Consistency: Branding and visual elements are applied consistently across all pages. (10%)
Accessibility & Responsiveness: Responsive web design (RWD) ensures usability across various devices and screen sizes. (5%)


### Chatbot Performance (30%)
Accuracy of Responses: The chatbot provides correct, helpful, and context‑appropriate answers to common user questions. (10%)
User Experience: The chatbot is easy to interact with, communicates clearly, and provides smooth conversational flow. (10%)
Integration & Reliability: The chatbot is well‑integrated into the website, loads reliably, and functions without errors. (10%)
